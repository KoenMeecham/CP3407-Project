User story title: Add Delivery Fee
Other versions: Delivery charge, Delivery cost, Apply delivery fee

Priority Notes:

Future – Free delivery promotions

Description:
Users can see a delivery fee added to their order total so they know the full cost before placing an order.

Description-v1:
Add a fixed delivery fee to delivery orders only.

Description-v2:
Display the delivery fee clearly in the cart and checkout total.

Task 3 – Display delivery fee in cart/checkout summary, Estimation 0.25 days
