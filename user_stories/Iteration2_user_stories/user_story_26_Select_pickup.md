## User story title: Select Pickup or Delivery

## Priority: 10
Priority Notes:
Iteration 1 – Basic pickup or delivery selection during checkout
Iteration 2 – Remember previous choice and show estimated delivery details
Future – Scheduled pickup or delivery time selection

## Assumptions:

- Orders can store an order type field

## Description:
Users can choose between pickup or delivery so they can receive their food in the way that suits them best.
